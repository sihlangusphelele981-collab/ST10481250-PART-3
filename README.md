1.	Organisation Overview

Organization: U Studios
Industry: Fashion & Clothing
Location: Durban, KZN, South Africa 
Category: 4-star Clothing Store
Website status: New Development Required 
1.1 Brief History
U Studios is an emerging fashion brand focused on modern streetwear and contemporary style. The brand was established to provide stylish, high-quality clothing that reflects youth culture, creativity, and individuality. By combining fashion, lifestyle, and culture, U Studios aims to build a brand that resonates with young consumers who value authenticity and self-expression. As the business continues to grow, having a professional online presence has become an important step towards reaching a wider audience and strengthening customer relationships.
1.2 Mission Statement
To design and deliver stylish, high-quality fashion that empowers individuals to express their identity and creativity.
1.3 Vision Statement
To become a recognised streetwear fashion brand that inspires confidence, creativity, and individuality in young people across the world.
1.4 Target Audience
The primary target audience includes young adults between the ages of 18 and 35, fashion-conscious individuals, streetwear enthusiasts, students, young professionals, and online shoppers who enjoy modern fashion.
2. Website Goals and Objectives
2.1 Website Goals
The proposed website will serve as U Studios' primary digital platform. It will showcase the latest fashion collections, strengthen the brand's online presence, increase customer engagement, and allow customers to purchase products through an online store. The website will also communicate the company's story and values while helping build a loyal online community.
2.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The success of the website will be measured by monthly website visitors, online sales and conversion rates, social media traffic, average time spent on the website, and the number of newsletter subscriptions.
3. Current Website Analysis
At present, U Studios does not have a fully developed website. This limits the company's ability to showcase products, attract new customers, and compete effectively in the online fashion market. The brand's strong identity and growing demand for online shopping present an opportunity to develop a modern website that improves visibility, customer accessibility, and the overall shopping experience.
4. Proposed Website Features and Functionality
The website will include a Homepage, About Us page, Shop page, Gallery or Lookbook, and Contact page. Customers will be able to browse products with images and descriptions, add items to a shopping cart, complete purchases through a checkout system, and contact the business using an online contact form. The website will also feature a responsive design and easy-to-use navigation with dropdown menus.
5. Design and User Experience
The website will reflect the modern streetwear identity of U Studios through a clean and minimal design. The colour palette will consist primarily of black and white, complemented by gold or beige accents to create a premium feel. Typography will use Montserrat or Bebas Neue for headings and Open Sans or Roboto for body text. Large product images, grid-based layouts, fast-loading pages, and a mobile-friendly interface will create an engaging user experience.
The homepage will feature a navigation bar, hero banner, featured products, brand introduction, and footer with social media links. The shop page will include product filters for size, category, and price, while individual product pages will display images, descriptions, pricing, and an Add to Cart button.
6. Technical Requirements
The proposed domain name is ustudios.com, while suitable hosting providers include Bluehost, Hostinger, or Netlify. The website will be developed using HTML for structure, CSS for styling, and JavaScript for interactivity. Bootstrap may be used to improve responsiveness, while React can be incorporated for more advanced front-end functionality.
